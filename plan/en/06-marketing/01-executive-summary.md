## Proposed decision

StreetProof's marketing plan prioritizes one measurable pilot and one concrete buying decision. The initial recommended ICP is Indonesian commercial building operators with 1-5 sites and small facility teams. Start the pilot at one site with 2-4 non-emergency corrective categories. Campus may take precedence when sponsor, access, data permission, and buying process are stronger. Available materials contain no customer interview, traction, revenue, or willingness-to-pay evidence.

## Three main bets

First, the founder learns the workflow and buyer through direct conversations. P researches 15 named accounts and seeks six qualified conversations over four weeks, capping that initial work at 12 hours. One demo shows an incomplete report, AI draft, operator correction, assignment, technician evidence, and supervisor decision. Conversations seek a sponsor and decision process rather than reporter signups. If access fails, change introduction routes or segment before buying traffic.

Second, onboarding helps one account finish its first verified workflow. Activation means a supervisor accepts evidence through a permissioned real workflow rather than AI marking work complete. The eight-week pilot preserves baseline, shadow, limited live, and evaluation phases of two weeks each. Data controls, role access, recovery, and permission must precede real data. Measurement can use controlled observation and spreadsheets. Operator, technician, and supervisor burden must accompany timing results.

Third, reuse the same evidence for commercial decisions and subsequent materials. Test Rp1,500,000/site/month and a Rp5,000,000 total eight-week pilot fee as offer simulations rather than market prices. Public content follows repeated questions from three accounts; case studies and referrals await value and separate permission. One asset can serve sales, onboarding, evaluation, and offers without creating a large content calendar.

## 90-day priorities

1. Assign P, E, A, Q, available time, account access, and the cost ledger; combine P+Q and E+A for two people.
2. Run ACQ-01/02 to test ICP, messaging, and sponsor access.
3. Prepare readiness checklists, accepted/rejected evidence rehearsals, and event/cohort contracts.
4. Present pilot scope and offer; start only after sponsor, permission, data controls, and baseline method pass.
5. Compare baseline against limited workflow, including AI correction, evidence, false routing, reopen, and input burden.
6. Decide paid continuation, scope/segment change, longer observation, or stop. Preserve reasons and pending cases.

If preparation finishes within four weeks, the pilot can fill weeks 5-12. Late access moves the calendar rather than removing baseline. Proposed gates: at least 20% lower median active operator handling time, at most 20% correction, at least 95% required evidence, no worse false routing/reopen against matching baseline, no critical incident, and a concrete paid buyer commitment. Agree these before the pilot; small samples support descriptive conclusions only.

## Budget and claim boundaries

Actual budget, funding, runway, fully loaded CAC, margin, retention, and LTV remain unknown. S0 with no incremental cash is the default. S1 simulates a Rp1,725,000 monthly cap including a 15% experiment reserve; S2 is Rp5,750,000/month and opens only with available funds and commercial evidence. Marketing team hours are capped at six weekly for two people or ten for four. Labor/opportunity cost remains in the calculation even without ads.

Problem value may be too small for the test price. With simulated 100 tickets/month, five fewer minutes per ticket, and labor at Rp60,000/hour, time value is only Rp500,000/month. Do not sell positive ROI from that example. Quality or rework value needs additional evidence without double-counting.

## A 12-month state worth testing

Seek repeatable onboarding, known service cost, reconcilable matured cohorts, and buyers who actually pay if value is demonstrated. Three paying one-site accounts at year end equal Rp54,000,000 run-rate ARR at the simulated price; this is a sensitivity scenario rather than a forecast or annual cash revenue. There is no requirement to execute all 139 ideas, increase spending, or hire a dedicated marketing team.

P owns strategy and cost decisions. Q protects evidence and user experience. E+A support the demo and measurement. Start with the [strategic frame](02-strategic-frame.md), follow the [roadmap](09-roadmap-90-days.md), and use [measurement](13-measurement-and-governance.md) to decide whether evidence supports continuation.
