## Assessment basis

This assessment uses materials available on 20 October 2026: the product research report, the mapping of 27 goals and 71 feature units, the hackathon idea materials, and planning decisions in this package. They contain hypotheses, capability lists, and execution designs. They do not establish a running product, interview results, customers, revenue, partnerships, or hackathon registration.

The defensible planning phase is pre-revenue. MRR, ARR, active accounts, actual spending, and funding are unverified. Use zero only when the metric contract defines it and data exists. Unknown does not mean zero. Spend as a percentage of ARR is not meaningful yet, and CAC cannot be calculated.

## Proposed team and capacity

| Role | Marketing responsibility | Boundaries and dependencies |
|---|---|---|
| P: product/commercial lead | Discovery, qualification, positioning, offers, channel decisions | Person and available hours remain unconfirmed |
| E: application/backend engineer | Demo, event exports, instrumentation checks | Product work still needs a separate allocation |
| A: AI/evaluation engineer | AI limits material, corrections, evaluation | No accuracy result before testing |
| Q: UX/QA/customer enablement | Observation, onboarding, support, case permission | Customer user time must be agreed |

For two people, combine P+Q and E+A. Proposed marketing cap: six team hours per week, four for P+Q and two for E+A. For four people, cap at ten: P four, Q three, E two, A one. These are marketing work limits outside product building estimates, not an assumption that everyone works full time. Two people defer regular content and all paid experiments. P's strategic skills are unknown; first assign ownership and assess capability rather than immediately hiring a CMO.

[Acquisition](04-acquisition.md) therefore prioritizes qualified conversations, while [activation](05-activation.md) prioritizes the first verified workflow instead of increasing traffic.
