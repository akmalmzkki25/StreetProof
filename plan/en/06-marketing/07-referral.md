## Recommendation

Start with customer introductions after results can be discussed. Do not build an affiliate program or viral loop before an account receives value. In facility B2B, the beneficiary, signatory, and introducer may differ. P records all three rather than assuming every user may sell the product to other buildings.

Introductions are not a pilot obligation. Customers need not approve a case study, logo, review, or recommendation to receive contracted service. A letter of interest is not revenue. Hackathon exposure is also different from a buyer referral or product validation.

## Sequence after demonstrated value

| ID | Moment and action | Owner, skill, tool | Cap | Decision gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| REF-01 | After an accepted evaluation, ask whether a similar operator would welcome an introduction | P; referrals, customer-research; manual CRM notes | At most one request per account per quarter; no commission | If five matured accounts yield no introductions, review value and recipient fit; do not add rewards to cover the problem |
| REF-02 | Create a forwardable one-page summary with approved scope and results | P+Q; copywriting, sales-enablement; Markdown/PDF | At most two hours adapting an existing evaluation | Hold publication if permission, metric denominators, or result limits are unclear |
| REF-03 | Test one introduction from a suitable maintenance provider after conflict review | P; co-marketing, revops; account list and written agreement | One candidate partner, no exclusivity or commission before approval | Stop if the partner wants cross-tenant access, manipulated vendor scores, or unfunded integration |

Five accounts is a future review condition rather than a mandatory 90-day target. REF-01 may be inappropriate during the first pilot. Q obtains publication permission separately from operational data permission. Declining does not harm customer status.

## Shareable material

Use synthetic examples for unapproved materials. For real cases, state observation period, eligible ticket count, work categories, verification definition, and limitations. Do not display resident photos, number plates, private rooms, sensitive locations, or token status links. An anonymized summary still needs an identifiability review.

Introductions lead to a readiness conversation rather than a public link into customer data. P may use `customer_intro` and internal account IDs in a restricted CRM. Do not place introducer names, emails, or tokens in UTMs. If Google analytics is later used, its policy prohibits sending personally identifiable information; inspect campaign parameters and URLs too. [M-02: Google Analytics PII](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6366371?hl=en)

## Measurement and cost

Track eligible accounts, actual requests, permissioned introductions, qualified meetings, and paid accounts. `Referral30 = requested accounts providing at least one permissioned introduction within 30 days / requested accounts whose 30-day window has ended.` Show pending separately. Use one primary source and an assisted-source note so two introducers do not double-count a customer.

Referral cost includes P/Q time, materials, partner activity, and incentives if later approved. S0/S1 allocate no cash rewards, commissions, or two-sided offers. Before a paid program, P must approve conflict rules, recipient eligibility, customer organization rules, and payment records. These are program design needs, not a claim of completed compliance.

## Horizon and stop rules

90 days: prepare permission forms, case summary format, and source fields; request referrals only after acknowledged value. Months 4-6: test introductions from a second account if available. Months 7-12: consider implementation partners only if onboarding repeats and margin supports partner costs. Do not use certified partner without operating standards, assessment, and responsibilities.

P stops requests if customers feel pressured, results remain unproven, or the core service has problems. The next action is to prepare the permission sheet with Q, then wait for the value gates in [retention](06-retention.md) and [revenue](08-revenue.md).
