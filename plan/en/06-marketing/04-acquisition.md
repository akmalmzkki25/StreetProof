## Channel decision

No StreetProof channel has observed results. Select founder-led sales as the primary experiment. P researches accounts, requests conversations through already authorized relationships, and reviews one ticket workflow with prospective buyers. This document plans the work; it does not instruct automated messaging or outreach now.

A qualified account needs an ICP facility operator, repeated corrective work, access to an operator and supervisor, and a prospective buyer who can explain procurement steps. Count accounts rather than multiple contacts at one building. Contacts, meetings, demos, pilot candidates, and paid commitments remain distinct stages.

## 90-day portfolio

| ID | Experiment and mechanism | Owner, tool, skill | Cap and leading indicator | Continue or stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ACQ-01 | List 15 named accounts and seek six workflow conversations, including two campus comparison accounts if accessible | P; local spreadsheet/manual CRM; customer-research, sales-enablement | At most 12 hours of initial research and conversations over four weeks; S0 cash or travel within S1 | If fewer than three conversations follow 15 accounts and two authorized introduction routes, change access route; do not conclude the problem is absent |
| ACQ-02 | One-ticket demo: incomplete report, AI draft, correction, assignment, evidence, supervisor approval/rejection | P+E+A; synthetic demo and local recording; sales-enablement, copywriting | Two message versions across three conversations each; no statistical A/B test | After six conversations, retain the version buyers can explain with its limits; revise if four still think it is a public complaint portal |
| ACQ-03 | Pilot readiness sheet and baseline template answer observed questions | P+Q; Markdown/PDF or spreadsheet; content-strategy, copy-editing | Two assets, at most four editing hours per month; no daily posting target | Create a public article only when the same question occurs at three distinct accounts; stop a topic if six uses do not help a next step |
| ACQ-04 | Limited distribution through a founder profile or relevant community after access and rules are clear | P; manual posting after approval; social | At most two adaptations of existing assets per month; assess relevant responding accounts | If two months yield impressions and no ICP conversations, stop the routine and retain assets for sales |

Skills are available work methods, not extra staff. Six conversations are an evidence collection target, not achieved traction. P records date, role, source, follow-up permission, recent problem, and next step. Q separates recorded buyer language from team paraphrases.

## Demand-gated content

Start with one page explaining the workflow, pilot scope, AI limits, and a Review one ticket workflow invitation. Do not display unproven logos, ROI numbers, testimonials, or integrations. Provide a short buyer version and an evidence example for the supervisor. Once repeated questions emerge, choose at most two topics, such as reviewable closure evidence or measuring administration without equating elapsed time with effort.

Google recommends an audience focus and a clear site purpose; this supports deliberate editing rather than mass page production. StreetProof keywords, search volume, and rankings have not been measured. [M-01: Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)

| New community | Later | Practitioners want repeated interaction and Q can moderate |
| App Store, consumer giveaways, engagement pods | Skip | They do not reach the pilot buying unit and consume capacity |
| Hackathon PR | Conditional | Verify official participation/results; do not claim participant or winner status without evidence |

## Sequence and dependencies

Weeks 1-2: qualification and messaging. Weeks 3-4: demo, readiness, and one sponsor if successful. Weeks 5-12: keep recruitment light while the pilot absorbs capacity. Months 4-6: test one permissioned result-based asset. Months 7-12: add one channel only if the first produces qualified accounts and service capacity remains available. The [roadmap](09-roadmap-90-days.md) sets team limits.

Without pilot access by day 30, P reviews the campus fallback, problem, and introduction route. P does not fill the gap by buying traffic. Customer approval, data controls, and product readiness remain pilot prerequisites; marketing cannot bypass them.
