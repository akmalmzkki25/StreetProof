## Planning boundaries

Day 1 is the team's agreed work start, not an official hackathon date. The ten-day demo is product work; this marketing capacity excludes that engineering. Weeks 1-4 prepare access and the pilot. If all gates pass, weeks 5-12 can hold eight pilot weeks: two baseline, two shadow, two limited live, and two evaluation. Days 85-90 support the decision and plan update. If sponsorship or data controls are late, move the pilot while preserving its sequence.

S0 is the default with no incremental cash. S1/S2 spending requires actual available budget. Cap marketing at six team hours/week for two people and ten for four. Each customer session also needs agreed customer time. Do not schedule parallel pilots before one reveals service burden.

The week-two review decides whether the access route reaches the right people. If three conversations have not happened, change access before adding material. A two-person team defers ACQ-04 and retains conversations and readiness work.

|---|---|---|---|---|

Day-30 gate: sponsor and data owner, permission, user time, scope, data controls, baseline method, and offer must be concrete. If passed, record pilot.prepared before baseline and start measuring TimeToLive. This is not org.onboarded and does not start Activation14. Otherwise select stronger campus access or continue discovery. Do not start a real-data baseline merely to meet the calendar.

## Weeks 5-8: baseline and shadow

| Work/ID | AARRR | Owner | Output and leading indicator | Cap/dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pilot weeks 1-2: comparable baseline | Activation | Q+customer | Volume, categories, active time, evidence, false routing, reopen recorded | Low samples mean extension/no conclusion |
| Pilot weeks 3-4: shadow | Activation | A+Q | AI corrections, errors, additional burden, review time visible | No risky automatic routing |
| RET-01: blocker review | Retention | Q | Blockers have owners and actions | 30-minute review + one analysis hour/week |
| Cost and account-source ledgers | Revenue | P | Time and cash are attributable | 30 minutes/week; no LTV projection |

Marketing reduces content activity during observation. P keeps a light pipeline, at most one new conversation/week, if this does not displace the pilot. User observation and publication require separate permissions.

## Weeks 9-12: limited live and decision

| Work/ID | AARRR | Owner | Output and leading indicator | Cap/dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pilot weeks 5-6: limited live | Activation | Q+E+A | After baseline and shadow, record org.onboarded when site/roles/real data/technician/supervisor are ready; start Activation14, end TimeToLive; pending remains visible | Data controls, recovery, and rehearsal passed |
| Pilot weeks 7-8: evaluation | Retention | Q+A | Compare matching categories and windows against baseline | Descriptive results for small samples |
| REV-02: paid decision | Revenue | P | Named buyer and concrete commitment, or decline reason | Separate contract, invoice, and payment |
| REF-01/02 if appropriate | Referral | P+Q | Case/introduction permission separate from service | One request without rewards; may remain unrun |

Proposed gates to agree before the pilot: at least 20% lower median active operator handling time, at most 20% AI correction, no increase in false routing or reopen against comparable baseline, at least 95% required evidence, no critical privacy/safety incidents, and a concrete paid commitment from a named buyer. Read all alongside volume and technician/supervisor costs. Thresholds are neither industry benchmarks nor promised results.

## Days 85-90 and stop triggers

P decides continue, narrow scope, change segment, or stop using [measurement](13-measurement-and-governance.md). Stop live use for a critical incident, stop new-site recruitment when support exceeds capacity, and stop adding channels while pilot value remains unclear. Q preserves findings and pending cases without automatically converting them into failures. A valid 90-day output may be an evidence-backed decision not to continue rather than growth.
