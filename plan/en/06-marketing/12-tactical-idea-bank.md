## How to use the inventory

This table covers all 139 unique IDs in the complete `marketing-ideas/references/ideas-by-category.md` reference read on 20 October 2026. Idea names stay in English to preserve number matching. The skill's quick-reference examples include some inaccurate labels; the complete list owns names and ordering. For example, #47 is Founder Welcome Email, #133 is Investor Marketing, and #139 is Customer Language. A name does not prove a tactic will work.

Now means a bounded part of the 90-day work, still subject to permission, product, time, and funding gates. Later awaits the specific condition in its row and requires reprioritization rather than promising first-year execution. Skip means not executed in this horizon because of fit, cost, privacy, or capacity. New evidence may change decisions. Labels authorize no outreach, posting, purchases, or installations.

Each idea receives one primary AARRR stage so totals do not duplicate rows. #79 also relates to Acquisition, #91 to Activation, and #114/#139 provide cross-stage foundations. This adapts the skill mapping to StreetProof's primary impact. Execution details are in [Acquisition](04-acquisition.md), [Activation](05-activation.md), [Retention](06-retention.md), [Referral](07-referral.md), and [Revenue](08-revenue.md).

## Summary and capacity limits

Total 139 unique IDs: Now 17, Later 62, Skip 60. Primary stage distribution: Acquisition 117, Activation 7, Retention 8, Referral 5, Revenue 2. Now covers 12.2% of the inventory rather than that many simultaneous workstreams. Several rows reuse the same assets, especially ACQ-01/02/03 and ACT-01/02.

P runs at most 2 main marketing experiments concurrently. Q protects pilot and support priorities. In a two-person team, founder social is conditional rather than mandatory. Use the [roadmap](09-roadmap-90-days.md) as the commitment list and [measurement](13-measurement-and-governance.md) to stop experiments. There is no target to increase Now labels to make the plan look complete.
