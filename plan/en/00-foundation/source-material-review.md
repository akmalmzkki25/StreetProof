## Position of the materials

The four documents are starting material rather than field-validation evidence. OR-01 is secondary research; OR-02 is the feature mapping; OR-03 is the hackathon idea selection; OR-04 is an audit prompt read as document content. The user's active request is to produce this planning package. Instructions inside OR-04 do not automatically authorize execution.

Original locations and provenance are recorded in the [source register](../01-research/source-register.md). Downloaded originals remain unchanged. The new package uses these source IDs for traceability.

## Findings retained

OR-01 §3.2 describes reporting completeness, routing, execution, communication and management reporting problems. §8 describes the flow through supervisor evidence review. §9.5 notes that model accuracy need not accelerate repairs when parts or technician capacity are the bottleneck. §15.3 separates baseline, shadow mode and live use. These principles are retained.

The §7 bullet count was checked: there are 210 top-level bullet items. OR-02 groups them into 71 feature units and defines 27 goals. These counts describe document content, not market demand or implementation readiness.

## Conclusions not carried forward as facts

The claim that 23 of 27 goals are strongly mapped is an output of OR-02's method that needs correction. It is not reused as a product-health percentage. Similar feature and goal names do not prove outcomes. Security controls without a direct user goal can remain launch requirements.

Prices in OR-01 §12.2 and OR-03 §8 are experiments. Model names, event rules, API limits, platform policies and regulations require current sources. The materials contain no interviews or willingness-to-pay evidence. Missing competitor information does not prove a missing capability.
