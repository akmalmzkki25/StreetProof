## Baseline scenarios, not promises

Calculations use three simulated volumes: demonstration 100 tickets/month, pilot 1,000, and 10 early sites 10,000. USD 1 = IDR16,000 is only a sensitivity input. IDR1,500,000/site/month and IDR5,000,000 for an 8-week pilot are pricing hypotheses, not willingness-to-pay research. Do not present them as market prices or actual revenue forecasts.

## Handling unavailable answers

Record when evidence was sought, inspectable sources and what remains missing. Do not replace unknown AI cost with zero, unmeasured CAC with advertising CAC, or uncontacted customers with synthetic personas. Calculate scenarios only with clearly labeled assumptions.

When evidence fails, P chooses a narrower scope, another segment, a longer baseline or pilot termination. Record the change in the decision register and propagate it to both languages. Do not lower targets after observing results merely to make the pilot look successful.
