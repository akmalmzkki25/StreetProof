# Business model

Date: 2026-10-20 | Status: Proposed / research-based | Document ID: B-02

[English / Bahasa Indonesia](../../id/02-business/business-model.md)

## Recommendation

Test a site subscription after a bounded paid pilot. Charge for the operator's workflow rather than each reporter, because a reporter fee could discourage useful reports and several mature competitors already offer free requester access. A site is a defined operational location, not an unlimited corporate group. Do not promise unlimited AI or support before measuring their costs.

## Proposed model

| Element | Proposal | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer | Commercial operator with 1-5 sites; campus conditional alternative | Named supervisor, data owner, recurring eligible reports and buyer |
| User value | Less report reconstruction; traceable supervisor acceptance | Baseline/live effort, technician burden and rejection/reopen behavior |
| Revenue | Rp1,500,000/site/month price test; separate setup/custom integration | Concrete paid decision; renewal after observed value |
| Pilot | Rp5,000,000 total for 8 weeks, price test | Signed scope and fee acceptance; no assumed subsidy |
| Acquisition | Founder-led discovery and permissioned introductions | Qualified pipeline and fully loaded acquisition cost |
| Delivery | Small software team with guided one-site onboarding | Actual enablement hours and support capacity |
| Key dependencies | Customer authority, secure hosting, private media, AI access and reliable notifications | Account tests, data agreement, provider terms and fallback |
| Retention | Repeated use for eligible work with buyer-visible evidence | Cohort renewal, coverage of eligible reports and support burden |

Vendor facts about free requesters do not validate StreetProof's pricing. See the [competitor comparison](../01-research/competitor-landscape.md).

## Revenue recognition and value capture

Keep one-off pilot/setup fees separate from recurring subscriptions. A free site, unpaid invoice, expression of interest or hackathon prize is not recurring customer revenue. For management reporting, show contracted, invoiced and collected amounts separately. Recognized revenue and tax treatment require an agreed accounting policy; do not infer legal treatment from this plan.

Price should capture a share of demonstrated value while funding support and secure operation. Time saved is capacity value until the customer explains how it is used or converted to cash. Avoid monetizing every reopened ticket as avoided downtime. Only quantify avoided loss with a credible counterfactual and customer evidence. The base case in [unit economics](unit-economics-and-costs.md) intentionally shows that 100 tickets/month can produce labor value below the proposed price.

## Model alternatives and reversal triggers

| Alternative | Use when | Cost or reason to defer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standalone site subscription | No adequate system of record exists | Must own safe workflow, backup and support |
| Intake/export companion | Buyer already uses CMMS and wants better input | Integration rights, reconciliation and duplicated effort need proof |
| Managed reporting service | Customer demands substantial human triage | Labor scales with volume; quote separately and avoid disguising service as SaaS margin |
| ERP/CMMS reseller or marketplace | Partner agreement and commercial advantage verified | No relationship exists; defer until independent value and support responsibilities are clear |

If most value comes from manual consultancy, report a service business and its labor margin. If willingness to pay is lower than delivery cost, reduce burden, change the segment or stop; do not assume volume will repair negative contribution.
