# Commercial and procurement

Date: 2026-10-20 | Status: Proposed / research-based | Document ID: B-05

[English / Bahasa Indonesia](../../id/02-business/commercial-and-procurement.md)

## Proposed route

Use a short scoped pilot agreement before real customer data or paid delivery. The commercial building recommendation does not establish a particular procurement method. Campus/public-institution procedures and private-operator procedures must be confirmed with the authorized buyer; a small price does not imply procurement exemption. This is a drafting checklist, not legal advice or a claim of compliance.

## Deal preparation

| Item | Evidence or term to agree | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buyer and authority | Contracting entity, authorized signer, budget owner, approval path and timing | P + customer |
| Pilot scope | One site, 2-4 categories, preparation plus 8-week measurement, roles and excluded emergency work | P/Q + supervisor |
| Deliverables | Configured workflow, enablement, agreed exports, result report and exit handover | P/E/Q |
| Fee and collection | Rp5,000,000 test only; actual amount, billing milestones, tax/withholding handling and payment date in offer | P + buyer/accounting |
| Success and acceptance | Pre-agreed metrics, incomplete-data handling, operational readiness and buyer decision date | P/Q + buyer |
| Data responsibilities | Authorized purpose, notices, permitted data, media minimization, subprocessors, hosting region and access | E + data owner |
| Service operation | Named contacts, support hours, incident escalation, fallback, backup/restore responsibilities | E/Q + customer |
| Change and exit | Scope-change process, export format, deletion/backup timeline and subscription decision | P/E + customer |

No uptime SLA, response-time guarantee, insurance, indemnity, refund or liability cap is adopted by this document. Such commitments require capacity review and appropriate contract advice. Promise only a service the small team can actually operate. A draft fee is not an invoice, accepted quote or revenue.

## Data and operating terms

Use private media and tenant-scoped roles. Reporter status tokens should be random with at least 128 bits, stored hashed, scoped and revocable; no sequential public lookup. Proposed signed-media TTL is 5 minutes. Operational-media retention of 90 days and audit retention of 365 days are configurable planning assumptions requiring customer/data-owner agreement, not statutory requirements. Clarify object deletion, logs, exported copies and backup expiry rather than promising immediate deletion from every copy.

Confirm actual hosting region, provider terms, AI processing, access controls and any cross-border requirements before real data. Do not claim Indonesian legal compliance from an RBAC/audit/retention checklist. If the customer requires a review or assessment beyond team capability, keep the pilot synthetic until resolved.

## Buying evidence and stage gates

Discovery interest requires no commercial claim. A qualified pilot needs named authority, defined problem, access, agreed scope and a dated decision. A paid pilot needs an executed agreement and collection evidence under the agreed terms. Subscription readiness needs observed value, service readiness and a concrete paid commitment. Distinguish a letter of intent, purchase order, invoice, payment and renewal; they are different evidence.

If procurement takes longer than the planned pilot start, move the start date and preserve preparation. Do not begin unauthorized real-data use to keep a calendar. No outreach, vendor contact, purchase or contract execution has occurred in preparing these documents.
