# Partnerships and expansion

Date: 2026-10-20 | Status: Proposed / research-based | Document ID: B-06

[English / Bahasa Indonesia](../../id/02-business/partnerships-and-expansion.md)

## Recommendation

Earn repeatable one-site value before adding partners, cities or major integrations. A partnership is a proposed relationship until both parties agree responsibilities and terms. No property operator, campus, vendor, government institution, Meta or CMMS supplier is a StreetProof partner today on the evidence in this pack.

## Partner hypotheses

| Candidate type | Potential contribution | Risk and qualification | First evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Facility/property operator | Pilot access, workflow expertise, later site expansion | One enthusiastic site may not represent portfolio authority | Named sponsor, data owner, baseline and paid decision |
| Campus facilities unit | Diverse reporters and accessible learning environment | Student access may not confer facilities or purchasing authority | Authorized facilities sponsor and buying route |
| Maintenance service provider | Technician access and recurring work categories | Incentives may favor rapid closure over verified quality | Shared proof/rejection rules and clear labor responsibility |
| CMMS/ERP implementer | Data mapping and a complementary intake route | Existing portal may solve the need; integrations may require paid rights | Documented API/export permission and support split |
| Property association/community | Introductions and domain feedback | Membership or an introduction is not an endorsement | Permissioned, relevant introductions and no invented logo use |
| Technology provider/program | Possible technical support or credits | Credits expire; eligibility and access are unverified | Official acceptance and account terms before budgeting benefit |

Do not announce joint offerings, use partner logos, imply endorsement or offer referral commission without a real agreement. A marketplace listing or technical API availability does not establish a partnership. Record conflict of interest and who owns customer data and support.

## Expansion gates

| Step | Gate before advancing | Capacity check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First site | Readiness, comparable baseline and scoped live pilot | Supervisor time and fallback route |
| Second site with same operator | First site demonstrates value and concrete paid continuation | Reuse setup; measure support and local exceptions |
| New operator, same categories | Repeatable onboarding, exports and positive contribution | Support hours/site and acquisition cost |
| New categories | Evidence requirements and routing owner explicitly defined | AI evaluation and technician burden per category |
| New region/sector | Buyer/data/procurement differences researched | Language, travel, hosting and support coverage |
| CMMS integration | Verified buyer need and rights, clear system of record | Reconciliation, retries, event IDs and maintenance cost |

At two people, sequence onboarding and avoid parallel bespoke integrations. At 3-4 people, add capacity only when workloads and budget justify it; do not assume full-time hiring. A 12-month capacity scenario of 2-10 retained paying sites is a target range, not committed pipeline. See [market sizing](../01-research/market-sizing.md).

## Commercial rules and next action

Evaluate partner contribution against delivery burden and lost direct customer learning. A reseller discount must leave contribution after support positive; a referral is counted only when a paying account is attributable under an agreed window. Do not calculate channel revenue from unsigned introductions.

P creates a partner hypothesis register with problem, contribution, authority, owner, cost, evidence and next decision. First prioritize one operator sponsor, then one repeatable second-site opportunity. No outreach or agreement is authorized by writing this plan alone. Defer broader expansion if the first pilot fails [validation gates](../01-research/field-validation.md) or [unit economics](unit-economics-and-costs.md).
