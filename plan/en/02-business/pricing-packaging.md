## Proposed price tests

Rp1,500,000/site/month and Rp5,000,000 for an eight-week pilot are hypotheses for testing, not quotes, validated market rates or approved budgets. No per-reporter fee is proposed. Do not copy a global competitor seat price into an Indonesian willingness-to-pay claim.

The Rp5,000,000 pilot test does not cover the full proposed delivery plan. The [WBS](../05-delivery/work-breakdown-and-estimates.md) totals 180-308 hours for readiness plus pilot support/measurement. At an assumed Rp100,000/hour plus Rp3,880,000 infrastructure, the funding envelope is Rp21,880,000-34,680,000, leaving Rp16,880,000-29,680,000 to fund before other costs. Actual hours and rates remain unknown. Separate reusable product investment from customer-specific COGS and approve the learning budget or revise price/scope before offering the pilot. The narrower 28-hour example in [unit economics](unit-economics-and-costs.md) is an optimistic sensitivity, not the selected plan budget; do not add overlapping WBS and monthly staffing valuations.

| Offer | Price test | Included scope to agree | Exclusions and boundaries |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hackathon demo | No commercial sale assumed | Synthetic photo/text workflow and scripted roles | No production SLA, real-customer readiness or acceptance claim |
| Eight-week pilot | Rp5,000,000 total | One site, 2-4 categories, baseline/shadow/limited live/evaluation, limited enablement | Customer internal labor, repair labor/parts, custom integrations, WhatsApp and unagreed travel |
| Starter subscription | Rp1,500,000/site/month | Agreed intake, operator review, assignment, evidence, supervisor decision, scoped reporter status and export | Setup, custom integrations, broad offline, emergency dispatch and new modules |
| Setup / integration | Separate scoped quotation after discovery | Data mapping, imports, agreed adapter and enablement outputs | No unlimited customization or migration promise |

Define included staff accounts, reports, media retention/storage and AI usage in a concrete offer after measuring pilot usage. There is no evidence to choose unlimited allowances now. Propose a usage notification and explicit overage consent rather than surprise charges. Site count is not reporter count. Keep security controls and data export available within the agreed service rather than selling necessary privacy as an optional upgrade.

## Testing protocol

P first checks the recent problem, monthly eligible volume, existing tool, buyer authority and current costs. Then show a consistent one-page scope and ask the authorized buyer to choose a concrete next step: paid pilot, dated purchasing review, or decline. Record amount, inclusions, taxes/withholding treatment to confirm, payment schedule, decision maker and refusal reason. A positive answer to a hypothetical price question is weak evidence.

Explore Rp1,000,000, Rp1,500,000 and Rp2,000,000/site/month as separate price scenarios. Small discovery samples cannot estimate a demand curve. Keep the same scope when comparing reactions; when a buyer requests customization, reprice scope rather than quietly discounting a different offer. Do not present these three scenarios as three finished tiers. Record any discounted pilot and its reason; a discounted acceptance does not validate full price.

## Buyer value and negotiation

At 100 eligible tickets/month, five saved operator minutes/ticket and Rp60,000/hour, estimated labor capacity value is Rp500,000/month. That is below the proposed Rp1,500,000 price. At those assumptions 300 tickets/month only covers the subscription before setup, technician burden and other costs. Price testing must therefore consider volume, measured time saved or other evidenced value. See the full [negative ROI example](unit-economics-and-costs.md).

Negotiate category scope, start date, training and implementation outputs openly. Any annual commitment, refund, cancellation or service-credit policy needs a real offer and capacity review; this draft promises none. A paid pilot can have independent value as evaluation and setup even if the customer declines subscription, provided deliverables and exit are clear.

## Decision and next action

Continue the price only when contribution after support is positive and a buyer commits based on observed value. Revise the model if low volume or heavy support makes both buyer and seller economics weak. P prepares the exact offer; E/Q confirm delivery burden; the buyer confirms procurement and data terms. No proposal has been sent and no price has been accepted. See [commercial procurement](commercial-and-procurement.md).
