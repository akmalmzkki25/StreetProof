## Recommendation and scoring

Keep this register open through the demo and pilot. All ratings are analyst hypotheses, not observed incident probabilities. Impact uses critical/high/medium; likelihood uses high/medium/unknown. Unknown is not low. P maintains the register, named owners supply evidence, and the customer owner controls customer data/operational acceptance. No risk is closed merely because a document describes a control.

## Stop and escalation rules

Stop affected limited-live processing for confirmed private-data exposure, unauthorized verified closure, unsafe emergency guidance, loss of required proof or unapproved personal-data transfer/training mode. Preserve a manual operational path only when it is safe and authorized. E contains technical incidents; A disables implicated AI; P/customer determine communication and resumption with advice as needed. Do not erase audit evidence or silently relabel an incident as a test.

Pause optional scope when the critical path loses reserve or monthly spend exceeds the agreed ceiling. Extend observation or choose no conclusion when volume/matching is inadequate. A buyer's interest without a concrete paid decision does not close R-01/R-03. Campus is a conditional alternate segment, not guaranteed access.

## Evidence and residual risk

For each row add actual probability rationale, prevention owner, due date, status, evidence link, residual impact/likelihood and next review date. Default status is open. Acceptance requires a named decision owner and a documented remaining limitation. Critical privacy/authority risks cannot be waived just to meet a demonstration deadline; keep data synthetic or remove the affected live scope.

Useful evidence includes a successful/denied cross-tenant scenario, restored DB+media reconciliation, provider account/terms probe, complete operator timing rows, reviewer-assessed routing labels, corrected proof history and actual cost records. A vendor feature list is not implementation evidence. [Security](../04-technical/security-privacy-and-governance.md), [quality](quality-and-acceptance.md) and [pilot protocol](pilot-and-roi-validation.md) specify those checks.

## Review cadence and next action

P reviews blockers on demo days 2/5/8 and weekly during preparation/pilot. Any new provider, media type, autonomous action, integration or segment creates a review of relevant risks before release. Assign actual people and customer decision authority before declaring readiness. Re-estimate schedule and budget from evidence; do not retain an obsolete date by dropping baseline, recovery or proof requirements.
