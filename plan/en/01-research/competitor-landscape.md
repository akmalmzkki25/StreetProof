# Competitor landscape

Date: 2026-10-20 | Status: Proposed / research-based | Document ID: R-05

[English / Bahasa Indonesia](../../id/01-research/competitor-landscape.md)

## Decision

Compete first against lost context and manual follow-up at a bounded site. For an operator with a functioning CMMS, assess whether an export companion adds value before proposing a separate system. The category has mature competitors. AI, photos, QR intake, dashboards and human approval are insufficient uniqueness claims.

The matrix deliberately avoids binary checkmarks implying equal depth. A vendor's approval step may concern request authorization, purchasing, signature capture or final ticket review. Those are distinct controls. Verify the exact close-out policy in a configured workflow.

## Common evaluation script

Use the same synthetic packet for each shortlisted system: an unclear leak report; two reports of the same issue; similar photos from different rooms; a missing location; a rejected completion; a reopened repair; and a reporter who must not see another tenant's records. Record operator actions, correction time, license/plan, evidence retention, export shape and exceptions. Test routine form rules before attributing benefit to AI.

Measure total effort, including setup and technician uploads. If an existing CMMS or form plus spreadsheet meets the agreed workflow with less burden, recommend that outcome. StreetProof should proceed only where its tested workflow creates additional value.
