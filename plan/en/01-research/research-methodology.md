# Research methodology

Date: 2026-10-20 | Status: Proposed / research-based | Document ID: R-01

[English / Bahasa Indonesia](../../id/01-research/research-methodology.md)

## Decision and scope

Use desk research to identify alternatives and design field tests. It cannot validate Indonesian demand, buying authority, product usability, or willingness to pay. The present recommendation is a commercial building pilot with one site and 2-4 non-emergency corrective-maintenance categories. A campus with a committed sponsor can replace it when access is demonstrably better.

## Evidence rules

| Label | Meaning | Required record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fact | A specific statement checked in a primary source | Original URL, access date, scope |
| Vendor claim | What a supplier says its product does | Vendor URL and plan qualification; no independent performance endorsement |
| Assumption | A number or behavior used for a scenario | Range, reason, sensitivity and validation owner |
| Proposal | A decision the team can revise | Rationale and reversal trigger |
| Target | A desired threshold agreed before a test | Denominator, time window and owner |
| Observed result | Something measured in actual use | Dataset, method, exclusions and uncertainty |

No interviews, customer commitments, installed product, traction, or trial-account tests were performed for this pack. Public marketing copy proves advertised scope, not successful implementation. A blank feature cell means unverified in the reviewed sources. It never means the vendor lacks the capability.

## Acquisition and limits

Research used agent-reach's Exa search and Jina reader. Exa returned useful official pages, then a free MCP rate-limit error (HTTP 429). Jina read official help and pricing pages; CAPTCHA responses for HashMicro, Total ERP, BPS and PDDikti were recorded rather than bypassed. Built-in web search/page reading supplied official-page fallback for the relevant blocked pages. No login, installation, vendor contact or social-platform survey occurred. Twitter, Reddit, Xiaohongshu, Facebook and Instagram channels were unavailable in the recorded environment. Agent Reach update check reported installed v1.5.0 current on the access date.

Prioritize official product/help pages, official statistics and government legal sources. Keep competitor-authored comparison articles as leads only. Their prices and criticisms of rivals are not copied as verified facts. Recheck dynamic plan entitlements and regional terms before purchasing. Record access date rather than inventing publication dates. See the [source register](source-register.md).

## Field sequence and decision rules

P recruits authorized participants; Q observes real report handling with consent; E verifies export and security requirements; A evaluates AI drafts against human labels. Start with recent examples and workflow observation, then a matched baseline, shadow operation and limited live use. Ask about the last actual incident before showing a solution. Include people who prefer chat, declined adoption, or already use a CMMS.

Compare active operator handling time separately from elapsed repair time, parts delays and access delays. Keep AI corrections, false routing, reopenings, technician effort and required completion evidence visible. Low volume yields descriptive findings or an extended baseline. It does not justify statistical claims. Use [field validation](field-validation.md) before making benefit claims and [market sizing](market-sizing.md) before describing market opportunity.
