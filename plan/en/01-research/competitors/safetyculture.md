## Commercial reading

The reviewed USD page displays Free for teams up to 10, Premium $24/seat/month annually or $29 monthly excluding applicable taxes, and custom Enterprise. It also offers location-based pricing; request the relevant model rather than assuming per-seat is the only option. [Pricing](https://safetyculture.com/pricing/)

## Comparable test

Create an inspection issue, link an existing open action and test the same-looking issue at a different asset. Observe access restrictions and whether an end-user can receive a limited status view. Configure completion rejection and record effort separately from inspection effort.

## Implication for StreetProof

For buyers driven by inspections, training and safety workflows, an existing platform may provide more value. StreetProof should focus on unstructured corrective reports only when that narrower workflow produces better measured effort. Never imply a safety certification or accident-prevention guarantee for either product based on a product page.
