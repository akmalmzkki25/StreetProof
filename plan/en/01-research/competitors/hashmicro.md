## Relevance

HashMicro is an Indonesia-facing integrated ERP/asset-management alternative. Its official asset page advertises maintenance and routing, so local relevance and automatic routing cannot be claimed as exclusive StreetProof advantages.

## Evidence reviewed

| Area | Reviewed vendor claim and limit |
| --- | --- |
| Maintenance | Official page lists preventive scheduling, work orders, maintenance team routing and progress tracking. |
| Asset scope | Asset registers, QR labeling, location/movement history, depreciation and cost visibility are advertised. |
| Suite connections | Page describes links to procurement, inventory, accounting, HRM and fleet; fit depends on actual contracted modules. |
| AI | Asset intelligence and conversational analytics are advertised; this does not verify unstructured report extraction or safe urgency classification. |
| Unverified | No-login intake, duplicate retention, compulsory supervisor proof acceptance, API limits and full local price. |

- [Official asset scope and FAQ](https://www.hashmicro.com/smart-asset-solution)

## Commercial reading

No numeric quote is adopted from the reviewed asset page. Obtain a scoped offer for modules, user/site limits, hosting, setup, customization, integration, support, exit and taxes. Avoid a blanket claim that an ERP is expensive or slow; actual configuration and existing contracts can change the comparison. [Official asset offering](https://www.hashmicro.com/smart-asset-solution)

## Comparable test

Ask a configured demonstration to carry one report through maintenance routing, parts purchase, completion evidence and supervisor rejection. Confirm whether this is standard configuration or paid customization. Export the event and media references to establish ownership and exit practicality.

## Implication for StreetProof

StreetProof's proposed advantage is a bounded workflow for a buyer who does not need suite integration yet. For an existing HashMicro customer, a configured module may reduce vendor count and data duplication. Do not repeat competitor-authored drawbacks as findings.
