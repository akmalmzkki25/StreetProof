## Relevance

PlanRadar is especially relevant when defects, drawings, project records and formal review matter. Its documented ticket approval applies to the whole ticket including attachments. Evidence-linked review is therefore established competitive scope, not a StreetProof-only capability.

[Subscription model](https://help.planradar.com/en/pricing-and-subscription/)

## Comparable test

Place two defects on a plan, submit a completion with attachments, reject the whole ticket and resubmit. Test whether a reviewer can assess the required category evidence and whether an external participant sees only authorized records. Time setup of the drawing and reviewer rules.

## Implication for StreetProof

StreetProof's manual location can reduce drawing setup for a simple corrective pilot, but loses spatial precision that some buyers value. If drawing-based defect handover is central, evaluate PlanRadar first. A narrow StreetProof export should preserve external IDs and avoid asserting that a ticket approval is legally sufficient handover.
