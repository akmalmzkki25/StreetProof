Date: 2026-10-20 | Status: Proposed / research-based | Document ID: C-06

[English / Bahasa Indonesia](../../../id/01-research/competitors/infraspeak.md)

## Relevance

Infraspeak is a facilities-management platform alternative rather than merely a maintenance database. Its official communication page explicitly includes end-user requests, making a simplistic claim that incumbent systems ignore reporters inaccurate.

## Evidence reviewed

| Area | Reviewed vendor claim and limit |
| --- | --- |
| End users | Infraspeak Direct lets end users such as reception/security submit maintenance requests in the app. |
| Communication | Notes, comments and notifications are advertised, including updates when work orders close. |
| Platform | Pricing page describes native apps, Hub integrations and connections to ERP/CRM/BMS/IoT. |
| Intelligence | Gear suggestions, alerts and automation are vendor claims, not independent performance evidence. |
| Unverified | No-login token behavior, exact merge semantics, site quote and Indonesia-specific service availability. |

## Commercial reading

The reviewed pricing page invites a tailored solution/demo and does not supply an adopted fixed site quote. Its savings percentages and fast implementation statements are vendor marketing claims. Do not use them as StreetProof targets or as evidence of an Indonesian buyer's ROI. [Pricing](https://infraspeak.com/en/pricing)

## Comparable test

Use a receptionist report with missing context, a technician completion and an end-user status notification. Check whether an existing ERP connection removes duplicate entry. Compare request setup, permission configuration and proof-review effort, including required native apps and roles.

## Implication for StreetProof

StreetProof may suit a buyer who needs only one bounded workflow and can tolerate a smaller feature set. If the site needs vendor coordination, connected assets or broad FM processes now, a mature platform may fit better. An integration opportunity requires verified API rights and a commercial relationship; neither exists yet.
