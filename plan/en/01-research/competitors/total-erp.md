Date: 2026-10-20 | Status: Proposed / research-based | Document ID: C-08

[English / Bahasa Indonesia](../../../id/01-research/competitors/total-erp.md)

## Relevance

Total ERP is an Indonesia-facing facilities/ERP alternative. The official facilities page includes complaint follow-up as well as scheduled maintenance. It should be evaluated as a possible configured solution, not dismissed as accounting software.

## Evidence reviewed

| Area | Reviewed vendor claim and limit |

## Commercial reading

No adopted numeric price is established from the reviewed official facilities page. Request a specific module and implementation scope before comparing economics. Public vendor articles can contain rival-price summaries that conflict with current primary pricing; they are not used as competitor quotations here. [Official facilities page](https://www.total-erp.com/software-fasilitas/)

## Comparable test

Demonstrate one tenant complaint, technician assignment, completion evidence, a supervisor rejection and cost reporting. Verify which behaviors are standard, configured or custom. Ask for a full export and data deletion process, and confirm the contracting entity and module relationship directly without inferring it from page branding.

## Implication for StreetProof

StreetProof can test a smaller corrective-work scope while Total ERP may fit a buyer needing purchasing and asset integration. A local vendor may also provide hands-on enablement that a small StreetProof team cannot match. No partnership exists.
