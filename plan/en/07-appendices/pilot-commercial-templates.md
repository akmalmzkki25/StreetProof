## Template status

Use this as a discussion checklist and offer draft. It is not a final contract, legal advice or confirmation of a prospect. Fill placeholders with authorized parties and obtain review before signing. Do not use organization names or logos without permission.

## Pilot scope sheet

| Item | Agreement required |
|---|---|
| Organization/site | [Legal name, responsible person, one pilot site] |
| Sponsor and operators | [Sponsor, operator, technician, supervisor, IT/data approver] |
| Purpose | Test administrative effort, evidence quality and input burden against baseline |
| Included | One site, 2-4 non-emergency categories, roles and volume boundaries |
| Excluded | Emergency dispatch, certified inspection, procurement/payments, unauthorized production integration |
| Duration | Preparation before week 1; 8 observation weeks under protocol |
| Data | Media types, minimal identity fields, source, permission and owner |
| Evaluation | Metric definitions, cohort windows, proposed targets and reviewer |
| Fees | IDR5,000,000 total/8 weeks hypothesis; clarify negotiation, tax and exclusions |
| Support | Agreed hours, channels, severity and escalation contacts |
| Pilot end | Evaluation, export, access termination, data return/deletion |

The example fee excludes customer labor, devices, connectivity, physical repairs and custom integration unless stated otherwise. Do not count the entire pilot fee as MRR. Renewal offers are separate from agreeing to participate in an experiment.

## Statement of work

Deliverables: one-site configuration, categories/checklists, role accounts, operator/technician training, baseline sheet, shadow mode, limited workflow, dashboard, evaluation report and exit procedure. Each deliverable names owner, relative date, customer dependencies, acceptance evidence and treatment of delayed dependencies.

Example acceptance: an operator processes permissioned examples; technicians submit proof; supervisors reject and verify; reporters view restricted status; export follows agreed fields. Functional acceptance differs from achieving savings targets. Do not promise outcomes dependent on technician capacity or parts.

## Data and security appendix

Record purpose, controller/processor allocation subject to review, hosting/subprocessors, support access, AI model use, transfers, retention, deletion, backups, incidents, data rights and portability. Initial 90-day media/365-day audit values require agreement and are not universal legal obligations. Standard model mode is selected for real data; Contributor changes require separate review and valid permission.

Deletion covers active copies, objects, exports, logs and backup lifecycle. Do not promise instant deletion from all backups where providers cannot support it. Short-lived signed URLs reduce exposure but do not replace authorization. Follow [security-privacy-and-governance](../04-technical/security-privacy-and-governance.md).

## Support, changes and exit

Propose realistic business-hour support rather than unstaffed 24/7 service. Define outage, unauthorized access, blocked functionality and usage questions. Distinguish response from resolution targets. Scope changes name costs, schedule and data-review needs before work. Customers retain manual operating channels during outages.

The exit pack includes reports/tickets, duplicate relations, events, evidence metadata, authorized media exports and field definitions. Transfer uses controlled, expiring access. Record export receipt, account/token revocation, deletion schedule and residual contacts. Exclude secrets and other tenants' data.

## Buying decision sheet

Allowed statuses: not offered, received for review, declined with reason, non-binding LOI, signed paid contract, invoice and payment received. Record date, actually agreed value, conditions, authorized party and next step. No status is pre-populated in this package.
